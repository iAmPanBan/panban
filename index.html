01 About

I am a web app developer who also does all things IT. That means I can take a product from a blank repository to a deployed, maintained system — and then keep the machines, networks and accounts behind it healthy too.

Most of my work sits in two places. On the product side I build applications in TypeScript and React — mentorship platforms, interview prep tools, learning systems, finance dashboards and internal trackers. On the client side I build fast, accessible websites for firms, studios and small businesses, in plain HTML and CSS or on WordPress when the client needs to edit it themselves.

I work end to end: design implementation, front-end engineering, deployment on Vercel or Netlify, and the IT support that keeps it all standing. I am open to collaborations and remote work.
